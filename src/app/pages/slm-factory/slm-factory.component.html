The problem

Small models are the smart bet. Choosing one is still a coin flip.

The economics of small language models are no longer in question, the selection process is. Most teams pick a model off a leaderboard screenshot, discover it's gated or unavailable for inference three weeks in, and never run a single structured human evaluation before production. SLM Factory exists to replace that with evidence.

01

Leaderboard roulette

Public benchmarks compare models you can't run, on tasks you don't have. A score without your context is trivia, not evidence.

02

"Available" isn't available

A model can look perfect on paper and still be gated, blocked, or missing an inference provider. Nobody checks until the integration fails.

03

Benchmarks miss what humans catch

Tone, refusals, formatting drift, hallucination under your prompts, the failure modes that hurt in production don't show up in an MMLU score.

04

Evaluation lives in spreadsheets

Catalog tabs, benchmark screenshots, Slack threads of vibes. No shared record of why a model was chosen, or rejected.

Do It Once

One pipeline from "what's out there" to "ship this one."

Every candidate model moves through the same five gates. What comes out the other side isn't an opinion, it's a shortlist with evidence attached, rolled up into one dashboard.

STEP 01 · CATALOG

Discover

Browse every candidate in one place

A browsable catalog synced straight from Hugging Face. Model names, IDs and metadata stay current automatically.

  • Metadata synced from the Hugging Face Hub
  • One entry point for every model under consideration
  • Built for the small-model class, not buried among 400B giants
STEP 02 · LEADERBOARD

Compare

Shortlist in minutes, not meetings

Score candidates against each other on a curated benchmark set, side by side.

  • Rankings across 19 hand-verified model IDs
  • Like-for-like comparison within the small-model class

Scores are a curated, fixed dataset maintained by our team, deliberately stable for apples-to-apples comparison, not live-scraped. Live benchmark ingestion is on the roadmap.

STEP 03 · ADVISOR

Recommend

Synthesis instead of cross-referencing

An LLM-powered advisor reads your catalog and benchmark data for you and returns a grounded recommendation.

  • Grounded in the catalog + leaderboard, not vibes
  • Tied to your company and project context
  • A starting shortlist in one question instead of one sprint
STEP 04 · PROBE

Verify

Go / no-go before a line of code

The inference probe checks whether the model is actually usable on Hugging Face, accessible, not gated, provider enabled.

  • Catches the "great on paper, dead on arrival" models early
  • One click from catalog entry to go/no-go signal
  • The gap between promising and deployable, surfaced up front
STEP 05 · TEST SESSIONS

Validate

A human in the loop, on the record

Structured test sessions with a human reviewer, because the failure modes that matter don't show up in a benchmark score.

  • Human-reviewed sessions, not just automated scoring
  • Qualitative issues caught before commitment, not after launch
  • A recorded verdict per model, per session
ROLLUP · DASHBOARD

One view

Everything in a single place

Company context, catalog, advisor output and evaluation state, together on one dashboard.

  • Set up your company / project once, every view populates from it
  • The shared record of why a model was chosen, or rejected
